If you like comparing time zones side by side but want it in a native app — on your phone, your Lock Screen and your Mac menu bar — GlobeTempus+ is built for that.

The same idea, built for your phone

World Time Buddy made the side-by-side time zone grid popular in the browser. GlobeTempus+ takes that idea to iPhone, iPad and Mac: every city on one timeline you swipe with a finger, with the cities’ skylines turning from day to night.

What GlobeTempus+ adds

Working hours per location

Set each city’s working days and hours once; live ACTIVE and SOON badges show who you can reach.

Your calendar on the timeline

Events line up with every clock, and you can create a meeting where you found the slot.

Public holidays

A HOLIDAY badge for 240+ countries, plus a warning before you book on someone’s day off.

People

Colleagues who accept your invitation share their busy time on their card. Only busy blocks, never titles.

Alarms in their time

Set 9:00 in New York and it rings at 9:00 there, wherever you are, skipping their holidays.

Widgets and the Mac menu bar

World clocks on your Home and Lock Screen, and your cities next to the clock on Mac.

No account, no server

There is nothing to sign up for. Your locations stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud, your calendar never leaves your devices, and the clocks work offline.

Which tool fits depends on how you work: a browser tab you open on a laptop, or an app that is already on your phone, your Lock Screen and your Mac.

Questions

Is GlobeTempus+ a good World Time Buddy alternative on iPhone?

If you want a native iPhone, iPad and Mac app rather than a website, yes: it shows every city on one swipeable timeline, adds working hours, public holidays and your calendar, and includes widgets and a Mac menu bar clock.

Is it free?

It is free to download with two locations, three people and one alarm. A monthly, yearly (with a one-week free trial) or one-time lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited locations, people and alarms plus event details and creation, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Does it work on Mac and the web?

There is a native Mac app that lives in the menu bar (macOS 27 or later, Apple silicon) and syncs with iPhone and iPad via iCloud. There is no web version.
