Questions

How do I show multiple time zones in the Mac menu bar?

Install GlobeTempus+ from the App Store, add the cities you need, and choose which of them appear in the menu bar. Clicking the menu bar item opens the full world clock.

What are the requirements?

The Mac app needs macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Do my cities sync with my iPhone?

Yes. Your locations sync between Mac, iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account, with no sign-up.
